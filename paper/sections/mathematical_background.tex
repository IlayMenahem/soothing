\section*{Mathematical Background}
Let $\mathbb{K}[\mathbf{x}]$ denote the polynomial ring in variables $\mathbf{x} = (x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ over a field $\mathbb{K}$, typically $\mathbb{F}_q$, $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. A polynomial system is defined by a map $F: \mathbb{K}^n \to \mathbb{K}^m$ with components $f_1, \ldots, f_m \in \mathbb{K}[\mathbf{x}]$. The locus of simultaneous solutions constitutes the \textit{affine variety} $V(F) = \{ \mathbf{a} \in \mathbb{K}^n \mid F(\mathbf{a}) = \mathbf{0} \}$.

In this paper, we will mostly work over the rational, real or complex numbers.
