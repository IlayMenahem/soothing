Table~\ref{tab:baseline-comparison}.  Unless stated otherwise, entries are
end-to-end wall times in seconds.  \textnormal{P} marks a partial run, which
reached the 60-second limit after finding at least one SAT branch, and
\textnormal{TO} a run that reached the 60-second limit before reporting any
result.  These are single measurements.

signed distance $n=12$ refines to 924 hitting sets in 23.2 seconds and positive
affine $(12,10,6)$ to 833 in 4.7 seconds.
