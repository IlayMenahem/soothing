\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% Write the problem we solved and how
We introduced a solver-agnostic framework that decomposes polynomial systems
into minimal hitting sets of their factors. It prunes branches with certified
UNSAT cores and splits timed-out branches into the cases $x=0$ and $x\neq0$.

% What we proved about our method
We proved that refinement and variable zeroing terminate and preserve the full
solution set. The output is complete whenever all branches are resolved.

% Establish the novelty of our approach
The method combines a novel yet simple method for breaking down polynomial
systems into smaller subproblems with factorization and interreduce.
We add reusable UNSAT cores, and zero/nonzero splitting without depending on a
particular branch solver.

% Experimental results
We shown the method's effectiveness on benchmark systems and the larger the
system, the larger the speedup. additionally, we showed that the method is
the only one that can solve a QFT system in a reasonable time frame.

% Future work
Future work will consider better case splitting strategies besides variable zeroing, and better ways to keep track and generate UNSAT cores.
